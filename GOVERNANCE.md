# Governance

## Maintainers

The maintainers are listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). They review and merge pull requests, decide on RFCs, cut releases, and enforce the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## How decisions are made

- **Everyday changes** (fixes, tests, docs, tooling) merge once a maintainer approves them and the checks pass.
- **Changes to the language** need an accepted RFC first. See [rfcs/README.md](rfcs/README.md). An RFC is accepted when the maintainers agree, after it has been open for comment for at least 7 days.
- **Changes to these governance rules, the license or the ground rules in CONTRIBUTING.md** need the agreement of every maintainer.

Maintainers aim for consensus. If they can't reach it, the decision waits; the status quo stands until they agree.

## Becoming a maintainer

A contributor who has made sustained, careful contributions may be invited by the existing maintainers, by unanimous agreement. A maintainer may step down at any time by opening a pull request that removes them from MAINTAINERS.md.

## The specification

The specification is MIT licensed. Anyone may implement it, in any language, for any purpose, without permission. The conformance suite defines what passing means for each version. [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md) explains how implementations may describe themselves.
